# T1 — TCGA bulk closure

## Sessions

Run one session per script:

1. src/t1_bulk/01_build_scores.py
2. src/t1_bulk/02_deconvolve.py
3. src/t1_bulk/03_partial_corr.py

## Inputs and score contract

The TCGA Toil RSEM archive must already be present under data/raw/tcga/. No session downloads or modifies it. The score builder writes scores.tsv with sample, score_type, method, score, n_genes_used. It produces family GSDMA–E scores, GSDMD/GSDME single-gene z scores, and a separate ssGSEA method selected by --method.

Before running, freeze in config/params.yaml the z-standardization scope (pan-cancer or cancer-within), ssGSEA implementation/version, gene-set version, missing-gene rule, and whether cancer strata are pooled or adjusted.

## Deconvolution and partial correlation

Use immunedeconv with quantiseq, EPIC, and MCP-counter. CIBERSORTx is optional revalidation only. The partial-correlation session controls myeloid proxies CD68/PTPRC and endothelial proxies PECAM1/VWF, and bootstraps 1000 times for the change in correlation. Report the raw and adjusted values for each cancer/immune-cell/score stratum.

## Outputs and acceptance

- scores.tsv, assoc.tsv, and fig5_attenuation.pdf/png are written under --outdir.
- Output rows equal the number of samples (scores) or the documented cancer × score × immune-cell strata (associations); NaN rows are under 5%.
- G1 is evaluated only after its primary summary, denominator, and effect scale are frozen: attenuation is at least 30% and the 95% CI for Δr excludes zero.
- If G1 fails, use the downgrade wording in the main project plan: retain member and compartment heterogeneity, remove overestimation/distortion, and shift the main emphasis to T2/T3.
